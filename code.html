About us

A neighbourhood spot for late plans.

Sip & Smoke is a lively late-night hangout on Auburn Road. There's a juice bar for fresh blends, a counter for shakes and dessert-bar classics, and a relaxed outdoor courtyard for shisha.

Everything starts with the drink: tropical fruit, creamy avocado, sugarcane and rich chocolate, blended to order. Stay for a plate, share a dessert, or settle into the courtyard with friends.
